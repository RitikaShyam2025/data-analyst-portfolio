
# Tamil Nadu E-Commerce Sales MIS Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

The **Tamil Nadu E-Commerce Sales MIS Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel reporting project developed to analyze e-commerce sales performance across Tamil Nadu.

The project uses a dataset of **500 e-commerce transactions** to generate meaningful business insights through KPI reporting, sales analysis, customer analysis, and interactive dashboards.

The dashboard is designed to support **MIS reporting, business performance monitoring, and data-driven decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into an easy-to-understand MIS dashboard that helps identify:

* Overall sales and order performance
* Monthly sales trends
* District-wise sales performance
* Category-wise sales contribution
* Order status distribution
* Payment method preferences
* Sales channel performance
* Top-performing products
* Customer rating and delivery performance

## 📈 Dashboard & Analysis

### 1. KPI Analysis

The dashboard provides key performance indicators including:

* Total Sales
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity
* Average Customer Rating
* Average Delivery Days

### 2. Monthly Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales performance across different months to identify:

* Monthly sales trends
* High-performing months
* Sales fluctuations
* Overall business performance

### 3. District-wise Sales Analysis

Analyzed sales across Tamil Nadu districts to identify:

* Top-performing districts
* Low-performing districts
* Regional sales contribution

### 4. Category-wise Sales Analysis

Compared product categories based on sales performance to identify the categories contributing most to overall revenue.

### 5. Order Status Analysis

Analyzed order statuses such as:

* Delivered
* Cancelled
* Pending
* Other applicable statuses

This helps monitor order fulfillment performance.

### 6. Payment Method Analysis

Analyzed customer payment preferences across available payment methods.

### 7. Sales Channel Analysis

Compared sales performance across different sales channels to understand channel contribution.

### 8. Top Products Analysis

Identified top-performing products based on sales performance.
